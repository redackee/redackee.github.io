# Meta Ads Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** {{ "now" | date: "%B %d, %Y" }}

## Introduction

Red Ackee Software ("we," "us," or "our") uses Meta (Facebook) advertising services to promote our products and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information in connection with Meta advertising campaigns.

## Information Collection Through Meta Ads

### Information We Collect

When you interact with our Meta advertisements, we may collect:

- **Interaction Data**: Information about your interactions with our ads, including clicks, views, and engagement
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address
- **Meta Pixel Data**: Information collected through the Meta Pixel installed on our website, including:
  - Pages visited
  - Products viewed
  - Actions taken on our website
  - Conversion events (e.g., purchases, sign-ups, downloads)

### Information Meta Collects

Meta may collect additional information according to their own privacy policies:

- Your Facebook profile information (if logged in)
- Your browsing behavior across Meta's family of apps and services
- Information from third-party partners and advertisers
- Device and network information

## How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through Meta Ads to:

- **Measure Ad Performance**: Understand how effective our advertising campaigns are
- **Optimize Campaigns**: Improve our ad targeting and content
- **Create Custom Audiences**: Reach people who have shown interest in our products
- **Build Lookalike Audiences**: Find new potential customers similar to our existing audience
- **Track Conversions**: Measure actions taken after viewing or clicking our ads
- **Personalize Experience**: Tailor our website content and offers based on your interests

## Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, a piece of code on our website that allows us to:

- Track conversions from Meta ads
- Optimize ads based on collected data
- Build targeted audiences for future ads
- Remarket to people who have taken specific actions on our website

The Meta Pixel uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your activity on our website.

## Data Sharing

### Information Shared with Meta

We share certain information with Meta, including:

- Website visitor activity (through the Meta Pixel)
- Email addresses or phone numbers (hashed) for Custom Audience matching
- Conversion events and purchase data
- User preferences and interests (if voluntarily provided)

### Meta's Use of Data

Meta uses this data according to their own [Data Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/) and [Cookie Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policies/cookies/).

## Your Privacy Rights and Choices

### Opt-Out Options

You can control your ad experience through several methods:

1. **Meta Ad Preferences**: Adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook settings at [facebook.com/ads/preferences](https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences)

2. **Browser Settings**:
   - Enable "Do Not Track" in your browser
   - Block third-party cookies
   - Use private/incognito browsing mode

3. **Opt-Out Tools**:
   - Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): [optout.aboutads.info](http://optout.aboutads.info/)
   - Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): [optout.networkadvertising.org](http://optout.networkadvertising.org/)
   - European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): [youronlinechoices.eu](http://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)

4. **Mobile Device Settings**:
   - iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking
   - Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

### Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Restriction**: Request restriction of processing
- **Objection**: Object to certain processing activities
- **Data Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

## Data Retention

We retain data collected through Meta Ads for:

- **Campaign Analytics**: Up to 2 years for performance analysis
- **Custom Audiences**: Until you opt-out or we delete the audience
- **Conversion Data**: Up to 180 days unless required longer for business purposes

## International Data Transfers

Information collected through Meta Ads may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country.

Meta has implemented appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your information during international transfers.

## Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect information collected through Meta Ads. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Children's Privacy

Our services and Meta Ads are not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" above.
